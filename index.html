The label

About

Cyber Music Sweden is an independent label from Sweden, built for heavy music with something to say: nu-metal, alt-metal, rap rock and trap metal that hits hard and still feels personal.

We keep the roster small on purpose. Every artist has a voice you'd know in two seconds and a world of their own, and every release is built around one thing: a chorus that sticks.

We also take the work off artists' hands, from production and release planning to the Meta campaigns that put a new song in front of the right people. You make the music. We make sure it gets heard.
